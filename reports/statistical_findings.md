# Statistical Findings — Collections Recovery

## Objective
Assess the claim that collections recovery improved by 11% month-on-month.

## Results
Gross successful payment amount increased by 11.11% from February 2026 to March 2026, from approximately 170.28 million to 189.19 million. However, it fell by 7.38% in April, increased by 5.20% in May, fell by 4.60% in June, and increased by 6.48% in July.

Three of the six month-on-month comparisons from February through July were positive; three were negative.

## Conclusion
The data supports an 11.11% increase in gross successful payment amount in March compared with February. It does not establish a sustained 11% month-on-month improvement in the recovery rate.

## Limitations
The analysis uses gross successful-payment amounts and does not adjust for eligible outstanding balances or net reversals. It also does not establish statistical significance or causal impact. August was excluded from full-month comparisons because only partial-month data was available.

## Further Work
Define and calculate a consistent recovery-rate denominator, reconcile duplicate payment IDs and reversed transactions, validate account and borrower attribution, and evaluate campaign effectiveness using comparable target and control groups.
