# Executive Summary: Collections Recovery Analysis

## Objective
Assess the claim that collections recovery improved by 11% month-on-month using the available payment and campaign data.

## Key Findings
1. Gross successful payment amounts increased from approximately 170.28 million in February 2026 to 189.19 million in March 2026, an increase of 11.11%.
2. This increase was not sustained in April, when gross successful payment amounts declined to approximately 175.23 million.
3. The staged payment dataset contains 25,014 rows. Of these, 382 (1.53%) have missing payment references, and 14 payment IDs appear more than once.
4. Of 45,000 targeting rows, 36,139 (80.31%) fall outside their associated campaign date windows and require investigation.
5. August data covers only the first eight days of the month and should not be compared directly with complete months.

## Assessment
The available evidence supports an 11.11% month-on-month increase in gross successful payment amount for March 2026 compared with February 2026. It does not establish an 11% improvement in the recovery rate or demonstrate a sustained month-on-month improvement.

## Recommendations
- Define the recovery-rate numerator and denominator before evaluating the claim.
- Reconcile repeated payment IDs, missing payment references, and reversed transactions.
- Investigate targeting records outside campaign date windows.
- Validate account, borrower, agent, and payment attribution.
- Compare complete months using consistent reporting dates and a consistent account or outstanding-balance denominator.

## Conclusion
The 11% claim is only partially supported: March's gross successful payment amount increased by 11.11%, but further validation is required before concluding that recovery performance improved by 11% month-on-month.
